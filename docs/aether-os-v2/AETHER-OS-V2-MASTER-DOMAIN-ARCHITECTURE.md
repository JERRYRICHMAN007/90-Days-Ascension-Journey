# 0. Governing Domain Laws

Derived from Vision + Information Architecture. Non-negotiable.

1. **Domains are organs, not apps.** They share Season, identity, calendar truth, energy, and mastery language.  
2. **Primary object remains the Session of Becoming.** Domains create, protect, and compound Sessions — they are not replacements for Sessions.  
3. **Law of Minimum Domains.** A Season activates a small set deeply (typically **3–5**). This catalog defines the complete organ map; it does not authorize running all ten at full intensity every Season.  
4. **Law of Interconnection.** A change in one organ must be able to surface consequences in others.  
5. **Execution over intention.** Domain progress requires Session evidence.  
6. **Systems over motivation.** Each Domain’s heart is repeatable protocol.  
7. **Identity over goals.** Domain identity tags serve Season identity; goals are proof targets under that.  
8. **Scoreboard integrity.** Domain metrics must be hard to fake and drillable to Sessions.  
9. **Shrink, don’t skip.** When life compresses, Domain Systems shrink to faithful minima.  
10. **North-star filter:** Does this Domain design help a person execute their becoming — across interconnected Domains — with evidence that compounds into mastery and legacy?

# 1. Domain Catalog Overview

## 1.1 Canonical organ map (complete)

| # | Domain | Organ meaning (one line) | Vision lineage |
|---|--------|--------------------------|----------------|
| 1 | **Body** | Physiological substrate — energy, capacity, recovery | Launch organ (Body) |
| 2 | **Mind** | Cognitive performance, focus, emotional regulation under load | Implied by deep work, reflection, interconnection |
| 3 | **Spirit** | Meaning, devotion, stewardship, non-negotiable inner orientation | Vision spiritual altitude + future Faith |
| 4 | **Career** | Vocational craft and professional contribution under constraint | Operator / Engineer / Founder personas |
| 5 | **Brand** | Market proof, reputation, opportunity surface | Launch organ (Brand) |
| 6 | **Knowledge** | Intake of models, language, judgment | Launch organ (Reading), expanded |
| 7 | **Wealth** | Resource stewardship, leverage, long-horizon solvency | Future Finance |
| 8 | **Relationships** | Bonds, trust, reciprocal obligation, belonging | Future Relationships |
| 9 | **Projects** | Creation portfolio — shipped artifacts and finished outcomes | Launch Software + Writing-as-craft, elevated to organ |
| 10 | **Legacy** | Active stewardship of long-horizon meaning and contribution | Vision Legacy altitude expressed as living organ |

## 1.2 Dual natures that must not be confused

### Projects Domain vs Project object

- **Project (object):** Mid-stack artifact/outcome under Journey (IA Object Hierarchy). Any Domain may host Projects (e.g., Body: 12-week strength block).  
- **Projects Domain (organ):** The life arena whose mission is **creation and shipping** — the portfolio OS for deliberate makes. It owns creation-centric Journeys/Systems and is the default home for craft shipping when Season selects it.

### Legacy Domain vs Legacy Architecture

- **Legacy Architecture (IA):** Remember-mode archive substrate — Season packages, mastery maps, read-mostly history.  
- **Legacy Domain (organ):** Active Systems and Sessions of **stewardship, curation, contribution, and meaning-keeping** in the present Season. It *feeds* Legacy Architecture; it does not replace Archive immutability.

### Knowledge Domain vs Mind Domain

- **Knowledge:** What enters and is structured (intake → models).  
- **Mind:** How the cognitive instrument performs (focus, clarity, regulation).  
Knowledge feeds Mind and Projects; Mind enables every Domain’s Sessions.

### Career Domain vs Projects Domain vs Brand Domain

- **Career:** Role, vocation, professional integrity, job/venture duties as identity-aligned work.  
- **Projects:** Making and shipping artifacts (apps, books, products, campaigns-as-builds).  
- **Brand:** Distribution and market proof of value.  
A shipped product may touch all three without merging them into one junk drawer.

## 1.3 Season activation rule

| Season role | Domains |
|-------------|---------|
| **Primary (in play)** | 3–5 selected; generate Daily Actions → Sessions |
| **Supporting (monitored)** | Not fully active; may emit interconnection signals only |
| **Dormant** | Persist historically; no Operate pressure |
| **Forbidden pattern** | All ten fully active with equal Session load |

**Wedge example (from Vision):** A proving Season might activate Body + Knowledge + Projects + Brand (+ Career or Mind as fifth) — Spirit/Wealth/Relationships/Legacy monitored or dormant.

## 1.4 Shared stack inside every Domain

Every Domain hosts the same logical stack:

```text
Domain
 └─ Journeys
     └─ Projects (objects)
         └─ Goals
             └─ Systems
                 └─ Habits
                     └─ Daily Actions
                         └─ Sessions
                             └─ Reflection
                                 └─ Domain Analytics → Domain Mastery → (feeds Life Legacy Architecture)
```

# 2.1 BODY

## Purpose

Provide the physiological substrate that makes every other Domain’s Sessions possible: capacity, energy stability, recovery, and physical integrity.

## Mission

Train and recover the body as a first-class system of becoming — not as a vanity module and not as an optional hobby.

## Why it exists

Without Body, interconnection collapses into fantasy. Sleep, training, and recovery determine deep work, brand energy, relationship presence, and career stamina (Vision Law of Interconnection). Body is the root support organ.

## Responsibilities

- Own training, recovery, mobility, nutrition-as-system (not diet theater), readiness.  
- Define Session done-definitions for physical work.  
- Emit capacity/risk signals to Mind, Career, Projects, Brand, Relationships.  
- Protect deload and recovery as first-class (consistency over intensity).  
- Maintain Athlete-persona depth without forcing every user into athlete identity.

## Inputs

- Season identity and non-negotiables.  
- Readiness signals (from Reflection, prior Sessions, future integrations).  
- Stress/load signals from Career, Projects, Relationships.  
- Knowledge inputs (training protocols, health models).  
- Mind state annotations (mood, focus) — non-scoring.

## Outputs

- Completed training/recovery Sessions (evidence).  
- Readiness / capacity Interconnection Signals.  
- Physical Artifacts (optional: PRs, composition milestones — evidence-bound).  
- Domain Mastery trajectory (capacity + integrity).  
- Principles (e.g., “deload is not failure”).

## Objects it owns

- Body Journeys (hypertrophy, strength block, conditioning, rehab).  
- Body Projects (e.g., 12-week strength block).  
- Training Systems, recovery Systems.  
- Body Session evidence types (workout integrity, recovery session close).  
- Body Principles and physical milestone Artifacts.

## Objects it consumes

- Season constraints.  
- Knowledge protocols.  
- Mind annotations.  
- Calendar truth / energy budget shared across Domains.  
- Career/Projects load signals (to shrink training intelligently).

## Metrics

- Session completion rate (training + recovery).  
- Planned vs executed session integrity.  
- Shrink-tier usage rate.  
- Consistency across weeks (not single-day volume).  
- Deload adherence when prescribed.  
- Miss patterns by weekday / load context.

## KPIs

- **Training Integrity KPI:** % of non-negotiable training Sessions completed (full or valid shrink).  
- **Recovery Integrity KPI:** Recovery Sessions not skipped when System requires them.  
- **Capacity Stability KPI:** Low variance collapse weeks (evidence of sustainable loading).  
- **Support Signal KPI:** Rate of Body-origin risk signals that predict other-Domain Session failure (for Coach learning — not vanity).

## Success criteria

- Body Systems run when motivation is absent.  
- Season victory conditions involving Body are Session-evidenced.  
- Deloads occur without identity collapse.  
- Other Domains can cite Body as support, not as competition for “who wins the day.”

## Daily workflow

1. Derive Daily Actions from training/recovery Systems.  
2. Execute Body Sessions (or shrink tier).  
3. Annotate RPE/mood/friction if useful.  
4. Emit readiness signal if miss or overload.  
5. Optional micro-Reflection.

## Weekly workflow

- Review completion vs plan.  
- Adjust volume/intensity via evidence (not program-hopping).  
- Align next week with Career/Projects load.  
- Weekly Reflection: body as substrate for the organism.

## Monthly workflow

- Block review: progress toward Project outcomes (strength, conditioning).  
- Protocol review if plateau (Principle 7).  
- Interconnection review with Mind and Career.

## Seasonal workflow

- Commission Body role in Season identity.  
- Set non-negotiables and victory conditions.  
- Mid-season readiness audit.  
- Season close: Body Analytics snapshot → Mastery delta → Archive package contribution.

## AI responsibilities

- Protect recovery; discourage intensity addiction.  
- Cite Session evidence for protocol suggestions.  
- Flag Body slip → deep-work / brand-energy risk.  
- Propose shrink tiers under Career/Projects chaos.  
- Never grant Body Mastery for logging without execution.

## Analytics

- Integrity curves, load vs recovery balance proxies, miss correlation with other Domains, Project milestone evidence.

## Cross-domain relationships

- **Supports:** Mind, Career, Projects, Brand, Knowledge Sessions, Relationships presence, Wealth (stress capacity), Spirit practice stamina, Legacy stewardship energy.  
- **Consumes stress from:** Career, Projects, Relationships.  
- **Feeds proof to:** Brand (optional athlete/brand), Career (stamina narrative — carefully).

## Rules

- Recovery is not failure.  
- No clock-complete.  
- Volume spikes must not dominate KPI over consistency.  
- Body is not a separate fitness app silo.  
- Nutrition theater without Session systems is rejected.

## Future expansion

- Periodization depth, sport-specific Journeys, advanced readiness integrations (evidence confidence up, standards never down), rehab protocols, longevity tracks — still Session-primary.

# 2.2 MIND

## Purpose

Operate the cognitive and emotional instrument: focus, clarity under load, regulation, and metacognition — so Sessions across Domains are possible and honest.

## Mission

Train the mind as a system: protect attention, regulate emotion without letting emotion overwrite the scoreboard, and strengthen metacognitive Reflection.

## Why it exists

Vision requires reduced cognitive load, locus of control, and Reflection as feedback controller. Fragmentation and calendar confusion are mental failures as much as tool failures. Mind is the organ that keeps Operate clear.

## Responsibilities

- Own focus/deep-work hygiene Systems (as mental protocols, not Career tasks).  
- Own emotional annotation standards (input, not score).  
- Own metacognition training via Reflection quality.  
- Detect anxiety/perfection loops that inflate intention objects.  
- Support “healthy tension between compassion and standard” (Vision §7).

## Inputs

- Body readiness signals.  
- Session friction annotations across Domains.  
- Reflection content.  
- Career/Projects overload signals.  
- Knowledge models about cognition (consumed, not owned as library).  
- Spirit orientation (meaning reduces noise — consumed).

## Outputs

- Mind Sessions (meditation-as-system, focus blocks-as-mental-training, regulation drills — domain-true).  
- Attention-protection signals to Home (clarity).  
- Metacognitive Principles.  
- Emotional context packages for Coach tone (not progress).  
- Mind Mastery (regulation + focus integrity — carefully defined).

## Objects it owns

- Mind Journeys (attention rebuild, anxiety-system install, deep-focus skill).  
- Mind Systems (morning clarity ritual, shutdown ritual, focus openers).  
- Mind Sessions and Reflection templates at mind altitude.  
- Cognitive Principles.

## Objects it consumes

- Body capacity.  
- Knowledge (CBT models, attention science — as inputs).  
- Cross-Domain Session miss patterns (Analytics).  
- Season identity (to reduce existential noise).

## Metrics

- Mind Session integrity.  
- Shutdown/clarity ritual adherence.  
- Annotation honesty rate (presence of friction notes when misses occur).  
- Correlation: Mind System adherence ↔ other-Domain completion.  
- Intention-inflation indicators (objects created vs Sessions completed) — organism metric Mind helps interpret.

## KPIs

- **Focus System Integrity:** adherence to defined focus protocols.  
- **Regulation Integrity:** use of shrink/regulation Systems under stress (not abandonment).  
- **Metacognition KPI:** weekly Reflection completion quality (structured, evidence-citing).  
- **Clarity KPI:** reduction of Operate overload events (fewer “everything is on fire” days) — measured via Session planning vs completion stability.

## Success criteria

- Emotion annotates; emotion does not complete Sessions.  
- User can run systems on low-motivation days with smaller faithful Mind/other Sessions.  
- Reflection catches zombie consistency.  
- Mind never becomes endless journaling without execution.

## Daily workflow

- Optional Mind Session (clarity/regulation) especially on high-load days.  
- Protect Now Session attention (signal to Home).  
- Annotate emotional context on completes/misses.  
- Evening: contribute to Daily Reflection.

## Weekly workflow

- Review friction patterns.  
- Tune focus Systems.  
- Coordinate with Body recovery and Career load.  
- Weekly metacognitive Reflection.

## Monthly workflow

- Attention architecture review: what’s stealing Operate clarity.  
- Principle extraction.  
- Check intention inflation across OS.

## Seasonal workflow

- Define Mind’s role in Season (often supporting; sometimes primary for “rebuild focus”).  
- Victory conditions only if evidence-checkable.  
- Season Reflection: mental patterns of the chapter.

## AI responsibilities

- Soften tone using emotional context; never invent progress.  
- Challenge intention inflation.  
- Recommend shrink-not-skip.  
- Facilitate Reflection with citations.  
- Detect founder self-erasure / shame spirals without dark-pattern guilt.

## Analytics

- Focus protocol adherence, emotional annotation overlays, cross-domain completion under stress, reflection cadence.

## Cross-domain relationships

- **Enabled by:** Body.  
- **Enables:** Career, Projects, Knowledge, Brand, Wealth decisions, Relationships presence, Spirit practice quality, Legacy curation clarity.  
- **Fed by:** Knowledge, Spirit.  
- **Risks from:** Career/Projects overload, Relationship conflict, Wealth anxiety.

## Rules

- Mind is not a second brain note garden.  
- Mind is not therapy replacement.  
- No Mastery for venting without Systems.  
- Meditation/journal Sessions need done-definitions.  
- Mind metrics must not pathologize normal emotion.

## Future expansion

- Deeper attention protocols, clinical-adjacent integrations only as evidence aids with ethical bounds, cognitive skill Journeys — never chat-as-product.

# 2.3 SPIRIT

## Purpose

Hold meaning, devotion, stewardship, and inner orientation — the seriousness Vision names as spiritual altitude without imposing theology.

## Mission

Make room for a life that is not only optimized but **oriented**: practice, reverence, service, or calling expressed as Systems and Sessions with integrity.

## Why it exists

Vision §7: stewardship, devotion, humility before long timelines, dignity. Legacy needs orientation beyond self-optimization. Spirit prevents the OS from becoming a soulless performance machine — while still requiring receipts.

## Responsibilities

- Own spiritual/meaning Practices as Systems (prayer, liturgy, meditation-as-devotion, service, Sabbath/rest-as-sacred — user-defined within ethics).  
- Own non-negotiable meaning anchors for Season identity.  
- Protect rest/Sabbath-like constraints from Career/Projects colonization.  
- Feed Legacy Domain with meaning narratives grounded in evidence of practice.  
- Remain plural: secular reverence allowed; no forced doctrine.

## Inputs

- Season identity language.  
- Body/Mind state.  
- Relationship obligations (service, family).  
- Legacy questions (“what is this for?”).  
- Reflections on meaning.

## Outputs

- Spirit Sessions (practice integrity).  
- Orientation Principles.  
- Sacred constraint signals (protected time/energy).  
- Contribution to Season identity coherence.  
- Inputs to Legacy narratives.

## Objects it owns

- Spirit Journeys (formation seasons, devotion rebuild, service arcs).  
- Practice Systems.  
- Spirit Projects (e.g., complete a formation curriculum; service initiative — if outcome-defined).  
- Spirit Principles.

## Objects it consumes

- Body/Mind capacity.  
- Calendar truth.  
- Relationship needs.  
- Knowledge (texts, teachings) as intake — often overlapping Knowledge Domain Sessions with Spirit intent tagging allowed via interconnection, not duplication spam.

## Metrics

- Practice Session integrity.  
- Protected sacred constraint adherence.  
- Season identity coherence check-ins (Reflection-rated, not fake score).  
- Service/contribution Session counts when in-play.

## KPIs

- **Practice Integrity KPI.**  
- **Sacred Constraint KPI:** non-negotiable Spirit windows not consumed by Career/Projects.  
- **Orientation KPI:** Season Reflections cite meaning without abandoning evidence.  
- **Humility KPI (qualitative):** presence of honest miss Reflections without identity collapse.

## Success criteria

- Spirit has receipts (Sessions), not only feelings.  
- Spirit can constrain other Domains ethically (rest) without becoming avoidance.  
- Users of any worldview can run Spirit or keep it dormant without OS breakage.  
- No engagement dark patterns dressed as devotion.

## Daily workflow

- Practice Session if System requires.  
- Honor sacred constraints.  
- Annotate meaning friction if relevant.  
- Do not let Spirit journaling replace other Domains’ execution.

## Weekly workflow

- Review practice integrity.  
- Examine whether work colonized sacred constraints.  
- Weekly meaning Reflection (short).

## Monthly workflow

- Orientation audit: is Season still true?  
- Service/contribution Project check if active.  
- Principle extraction.

## Seasonal workflow

- Optional primary Domain for formation Seasons (Student/formation personas).  
- Define practice non-negotiables.  
- Season close: meaning narrative into Archive/Legacy Domain curation.

## AI responsibilities

- Respect worldview; do not impose theology.  
- Protect sacred constraints in scheduling counsel.  
- Cite practice evidence; no fake spirituality scores.  
- Help articulate identity language when earned.  
- Never use guilt-as-engagement.

## Analytics

- Practice integrity, constraint violations by Career/Projects, correlation with Mind regulation and Relationship health.

## Cross-domain relationships

- **Orients:** Career, Brand, Wealth, Projects, Legacy.  
- **Supported by:** Body, Mind.  
- **Coupled with:** Relationships (love/service), Knowledge (sacred texts/study), Legacy (meaning).  
- **Risks:** Brand vanity displacing Spirit; Wealth anxiety displacing practice; Career colonization.

## Rules

- No theology mandate.  
- No Spirit Mastery for poetic writing without practice Sessions when practice was the System.  
- Spirit rest is shrink/protection, not skip-of-life.  
- Spirit is not aesthetic mysticism configuration.

## Future expansion

- Tradition-specific Journey libraries (opt-in), service Domains coupling, communal practice (multiplayer last/constrained), chaplaincy-style Coach modes — ethics-first.

# 2.4 CAREER

## Purpose

Run vocational craft and professional contribution: the job, the venture role, the duty-bound work that funds and expresses identity under real constraints.

## Mission

Protect professional integrity and edge without life collapse — boundary OS for Operators, Founders, Engineers-as-employees, Creators-with-clients.

## Why it exists

Personas Professional and Founder need a Domain for work that is not identical to personal Projects or Brand. Career is where “work expands to fill life” is fought with Systems (Vision Persona 2–3).

## Responsibilities

- Own career Systems: deep work blocks for job/venture, review cadences, boundary protocols.  
- Own Career Journeys (promotion arc, company milestone season, skill install for role).  
- Distinguish Career Sessions from Projects Domain shipping (related, not merged).  
- Emit overload/self-erasure signals.  
- Align Season goals with company/role milestones when relevant.

## Inputs

- External role demands (user-declared constraints).  
- Body/Mind readiness.  
- Projects portfolio status (when venture = building).  
- Brand reputation signals (opportunity).  
- Wealth constraints (runway, compensation).  
- Relationship boundaries.

## Outputs

- Career Session evidence (duty execution, deep work integrity).  
- Boundary integrity signals.  
- Professional Artifacts (deliverables) when outcome-defined.  
- Overload risk signals to Body/Mind/Relationships.  
- Career Mastery (reliability + craft in role).

## Objects it owns

- Career Journeys/Projects/Goals/Systems/Sessions.  
- Boundary Systems (hard stops, meeting hygiene as system).  
- Role milestone Artifacts.  
- Career Principles (“self is not residual”).

## Objects it consumes

- Body/Mind capacity.  
- Knowledge/skills intake.  
- Projects outputs when Career is building.  
- Brand opportunity surface.  
- Wealth runway reality.  
- Spirit orientation (calling vs grind).

## Metrics

- Career Session integrity.  
- Boundary adherence (protected Domains not erased).  
- Milestone evidence rate.  
- Overtime/colonization pattern proxies (via missed Body/Relationship Sessions correlation).  
- Deep work Session quality proxies (done-definition based).

## KPIs

- **Role Integrity KPI:** non-negotiable Career Sessions completed.  
- **Boundary KPI:** Body/Mind/Relationship non-negotiables preserved alongside Career.  
- **Milestone KPI:** Career Project outcomes evidenced.  
- **Anti-erasure KPI:** Coach/Analytics flag rate of self-erasure patterns trending down.

## Success criteria

- Career excellence without silent health/relationship collapse.  
- Founder chaos still yields protected personal board-meeting Sessions.  
- Career does not steal Projects’ shipping definitions or Brand’s market definitions.

## Daily workflow

- Execute Career Sessions derived from role Systems.  
- Honor boundaries (shrink Career before deleting Body).  
- Annotate overload.  
- Coordinate same-day Projects Sessions without double-counting evidence illegitimately.

## Weekly workflow

- Role review: what shipped for the job/venture.  
- Boundary review with Body/Relationships.  
- Adjust Systems for upcoming intensity.

## Monthly workflow

- Milestone audit.  
- Skill gaps → Knowledge/Projects coupling.  
- Compensation/Wealth check-in (consume Wealth Analytics).

## Seasonal workflow

- Map Season identity to career chapter.  
- Victory conditions tied to role/venture evidence.  
- Close: professional narrative + artifacts into Archive.

## AI responsibilities

- Flag founder self-erasure.  
- Propose minimum effective dose Career Systems.  
- Cite evidence for “you’re winning at work / losing the organism.”  
- Do not optimize calendar density as destiny (anti-Motion confusion).

## Analytics

- Integrity, boundaries vs other Domains, milestone progress, overload correlations.

## Cross-domain relationships

- **Drains/risks:** Body, Mind, Relationships, Spirit sacred constraints.  
- **Fed by:** Knowledge, Projects (when building), Brand (opportunities), Wealth.  
- **Feeds:** Wealth (income), Brand (professional reputation), Legacy (vocational contribution), Projects (resources/time allocation).

## Rules

- Career ≠ entire Life.  
- Meetings without done-definitions are not automatically Sessions.  
- Promotion theater without evidence is intention.  
- Career cannot auto-complete from calendar blocks.

## Future expansion

- Team lead systems, founder operating cadences, client delivery Systems, industry-specific Journeys — still organism-coupled.

# 2.5 BRAND

Operate market proof of value: reputation, distribution, audience trust, and opportunity surface — without reducing the person to vanity metrics.

## Mission

Run distribution and proof Systems so craft and character become visible in the world — subordinated to identity and evidence (Vision Brand organ).

Brand is market feedback and opportunity. Creators and builders fail when shipping is invisible or when metrics anxiety burns them out (Persona Creator). Brand closes the loop from making → proof.

## Responsibilities

- Own publishing/distribution Systems.  
- Own Brand Journeys (launch, authority install, audience rebuild).  
- Consume Artifacts from Projects/Career/Knowledge/Writing-like outputs.  
- Measure proof without vanity dictatorship.  
- Protect sustainability via Body/Mind coupling.

## Inputs

- Writing/thinking outputs (often Projects or Knowledge→Projects pipeline).  
- Market signals (user-entered or future integrations).

## Outputs

- Brand Sessions (publish, engage-as-system, offer Systems).  
- Distribution Artifacts (posts, essays, launches — evidence).  
- Opportunity signals to Career/Wealth.  
- Reputation Principles.  
- Brand Mastery (cadence integrity + proof quality proxies).

## Objects it owns

- Brand Journeys/Projects (launches), Systems (cadence), Sessions.  
- Offer/positioning Goals.  
- Distribution Artifacts index.  
- Brand Principles.

## Objects it consumes

- Projects Artifacts (proof of work).  
- Knowledge (taste, insight).  
- Career milestones (authority).  
- Body energy.  
- Mind regulation (metric anxiety).  
- Relationships (community, collaborators).

## Metrics

- Campaign/Project completion.

## KPIs

## Success criteria

## Daily workflow

- Pull from Artifact queue (Projects).  
- Annotate metric anxiety in Mind without faking progress.

## Weekly workflow

- Pipeline review: made vs distributed.  
- Cadence tune.  
- Audience/proof Reflection (evidence + ethics).

## Monthly workflow

- Positioning audit vs Season identity.  
- Campaign Project review.  
- Wealth coupling (monetization systems) if in play.

## Seasonal workflow

- Brand role in Season identity (“install dual brand”).  
- Victory conditions evidence-checkable.  
- Archive distribution Artifacts into Legacy Architecture via Legacy Domain curation.

## AI responsibilities

- Fight vanity metric spiral.  
- Suggest pipeline repairs (Projects → Brand).  
- Cite cadence evidence.  
- Protect Body/Mind when Brand intensity spikes.  
- No engagement-hacking counsel as core.

## Analytics

- Cadence, pipeline conversion, proof proxies (contextual), sustainability correlations, campaign outcomes.

## Cross-domain relationships

- **Fed by:** Projects, Knowledge, Career, Spirit (authenticity), Relationships (community).  
- **Feeds:** Career opportunities, Wealth, Legacy reputation, Projects feedback.  
- **Risks to:** Mind (anxiety), Spirit (hollow performance), Relationships (neglect).

## Rules

- Brand is not social media addiction OS.  
- No Mastery for impressions alone.  
- Distribution without Artifact underlying is empty (except deliberate Brand experiments with honest done-definitions).  
- Creator burnout → shrink cadence, don’t delete System.

## Future expansion

- Multi-channel Systems, offer engines, authority Journeys, selective analytics integrations — scoreboard integrity first.

# 2.6 KNOWLEDGE

## Purpose

Run knowledge intake and model-building: reading, study, research, and structured learning that feed judgment, language, and taste (Vision Reading organ, expanded).

## Mission

Compound understanding that becomes output — Knowledge unfinished if it never touches Projects, Brand, Career, Mind, or Spirit.

## Why it exists

Obsidian-class knowledge without life compounding is rejected by Vision. Knowledge exists to feed Writing/thinking, Brand judgment, engineering taste, Spirit formation, Wealth literacy, Relationship wisdom.

## Responsibilities

- Own reading/study Systems and Journeys.  
- Own Knowledge Projects (finish Book N; complete course-with-output).  
- Define Session done-definitions (pages with retention ritual; not “book open”).  
- Produce highlights/notes as **intermediate objects** that feed Projects — not as success themselves.  
- Track unfinished intake vs output coupling.

## Inputs

- Season curriculum needs (Career/Projects/Spirit).  
- Mind focus capacity.  
- Body energy.  
- Prior Artifacts gaps (“I can’t write this because I haven’t studied X”).

## Outputs

- Knowledge Sessions (reading/study evidence).  
- Structured notes/highlights (intermediate).  
- Knowledge Artifacts (finished books/courses with completion evidence).  
- Feed signals to Projects/Brand/Career/Spirit.  
- Knowledge Mastery (intake integrity + transfer-to-output rate).

## Objects it owns

- Knowledge Journeys (theme arcs: systems thinking, craft taste, theology study, finance literacy).  
- Reading/study Systems.  
- Knowledge Projects.  
- Library objects (resources) under Systems — not Domains.  
- Intermediate note objects (non-primary).  
- Knowledge Principles.

## Objects it consumes

- Mind attention.  
- Body energy.  
- Career/Projects demand signals (what to learn next).  
- Spirit curriculum needs.  
- Wealth literacy needs.

## Metrics

- Study Session integrity.  
- Completion of Knowledge Projects.  
- Transfer rate: Knowledge outputs cited in Projects/Brand/Career Sessions.  
- Intention inflation: books added vs finished.  
- Retention ritual adherence (if System defines it).

## KPIs

- **Intake Integrity KPI.**  
- **Completion KPI:** Knowledge Projects finished / started.  
- **Transfer KPI:** % of finished Knowledge units that feed at least one output Session in other Domains within a defined window.  
- **Anti-hoard KPI:** declining “added but never started” rate.

## Success criteria

- Reading is not a bookshelf trophy case.  
- Tutorial hell is visible in Analytics and challenged.  
- Knowledge Mastery requires transfer, not only intake streaks.

## 4.2 Matrix narrative (how to read)

### Body row
Body strongly supports every Domain’s executable capacity. Neglecting Body risks all; overtraining without Mind/Career awareness still risks organism via intensity addiction.

### Mind row
Mind enables clarity and regulation everywhere. Mind failure raises intention inflation and Session fragility across the board.

### Spirit row
Spirit orients all Domains toward meaning; feeds Legacy with purpose; constrains hollow Brand/Wealth/Career aggression.

### Career row
Career feeds Wealth and Legacy vocation; can prove into Brand; competes with Knowledge/Projects/Relationships/Body for attention; risks Mind/Spirit/Relationships when unbounded.

### Brand row
Brand converts Projects/Career into opportunity and Wealth; risks Mind/Spirit/Relationships via vanity and time; feeds Legacy reputation.

### Knowledge row
Knowledge transfers into every craft and judgment Domain; primarily feeds Projects and Brand; supports Spirit/Wealth literacy; competes for attention with shipping Domains.

### Wealth row
Wealth constrains risk across Projects/Brand/Career/Relationships; feeds Legacy provision; anxiety risks Mind; posture oriented by Spirit.

### Relationships row
Relationships sustain Mind/Body/Spirit; constrain Career/Projects colonization; feed Legacy and sometimes Brand community; couple to Wealth.

### Projects row
Projects feed Brand/Wealth/Career/Legacy Artifacts; demand Knowledge; risk Body/Mind/Relationships under crunch; receive orientation from Spirit/Legacy.

### Legacy row
Legacy orients Season choices across all Domains; does not manufacture their daily Sessions; consumes their evidence.

## 4.3 Pipeline circuits (multi-hop)

1. **Creator circuit:** Knowledge → Projects → Brand → Wealth → Legacy  
2. **Operator circuit:** Body → Mind → Career → Wealth → Relationships (protect) → Legacy  
3. **Founder circuit:** Body → Mind → Projects ↔ Career → Brand → Wealth → Relationships/Spirit (guardrails) → Legacy  
4. **Athlete circuit:** Body → Mind → Brand/Career → Relationships → Legacy (with Knowledge for mental game)  
5. **Formation circuit:** Spirit → Knowledge → Mind → Body → Projects/Career (light) → Legacy  

Season selects which circuit is dominant; IA forbids running all circuits at full load simultaneously.

## 4.4 Conflict resolution order (when Domains compete)

When Session load conflicts, resolve by:

1. **Season non-negotiables** (explicit).  
2. **Law of Shrink, Don’t Skip** on non-negotiables.  
3. **Body/Mind minimums** if organism risk signals fire (substrate protection).  
4. **Relationships non-negotiables** if neglect pattern active (human protection).  
5. **Spirit sacred constraints** if declared.  
6. Then Career/Projects/Brand/Knowledge/Wealth optimization.  
7. Legacy curation yields to Operate except at Season close (then curation becomes non-negotiable process).

This order is architectural default; Season may rewrite explicitly (e.g., peak competition Season elevates Body) but must restate victory conditions and risks.

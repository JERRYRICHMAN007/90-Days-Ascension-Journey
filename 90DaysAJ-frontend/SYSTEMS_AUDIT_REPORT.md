## D. EXECUTION RULES

### RULES FOR CONTINUING VS STOPPING

**CONTINUE when:**
- Making clear progress toward daily objective
- Energy levels sustainable
- Time block not exhausted
- No external interruptions requiring attention

**STOP when:**
- Time block exhausted (respect the boundary)
- Objective achieved (don't overwork)
- Energy depleted (quality > quantity)
- External priority requires attention (work, family, health)

### RULES FOR REVISING VS RESTING

**REVISE when:**
- Task is too vague or unclear
- Approach isn't working after 15 minutes
- Missing information or resources
- Need to break down into smaller steps

**REST when:**
- Energy depleted (take 5-10 min break)
- Mental fatigue affecting quality
- Completed a major milestone (celebrate, then rest)
- Between time blocks (natural rest periods)

### RULES FOR PROTECTING HIGH-PRIORITY TIME BLOCKS

**High-Priority Blocks (Non-Negotiable):**
1. **Software Engineering** (Mon-Fri mornings, Fri evening, Sat revisions)
2. **Body Transformation** (Mon-Fri 5:30-6:30 AM)
3. **Dual Brand** (Mon-Fri 4:45-5:30 AM, Sat 5:00-6:00 AM)

**Protection Rules:**
- No meetings scheduled during these blocks
- Phone on Do Not Disturb
- No social media or distractions
- If interrupted, document and resume immediately after
- If missed, make up within 24 hours or adjust next day's schedule

**Flexible Blocks (Can Adjust):**
- Reading (can shift within day)
- Writer's Journey (can move to different time if needed)

## F. FAILURE POINTS & LEAKS

### IDENTIFIED FAILURE POINTS

1. **Time Spent Without Progress:**
   - **Risk:** Reading without note-taking = no documented learning
   - **Fix:** Require minimum takeaways per session
   
   - **Risk:** Learning without implementation = knowledge not applied
   - **Fix:** Require implementation within 48 hours of learning

2. **Tasks Too Vague:**
   - **Risk:** "Work on Transport App" = no clear objective
   - **Fix:** Always specify component/feature being built
   
   - **Risk:** "Create content" = no quality criteria
   - **Fix:** Define brand voice, CTA, optimization requirements

3. **Context Switching Reduces Effectiveness:**
   - **Risk:** Switching between disciplines too quickly
   - **Fix:** Respect time block boundaries, complete one before starting next
   
   - **Risk:** Interruptions during high-priority blocks
   - **Fix:** Protect blocks, use Do Not Disturb, document interruptions

4. **Goals Lack Feedback Loops:**
   - **Risk:** No measurement = no improvement
   - **Fix:** Weekly reviews, metrics tracking, adjustment mechanisms
   
   - **Risk:** No validation of task effectiveness
   - **Fix:** Analytics reviews, A/B testing, performance metrics

### ENERGY & SUSTAINABILITY RISKS

**Identified Risks:**
1. **Early Morning Blocks (4:45-6:30 AM):**
   - Requires 4:00-4:30 AM wake-up
   - **Risk:** Sleep deprivation if not sleeping by 9:00-10:00 PM
   - **Fix:** Define sleep schedule (target: 7-8 hours)

2. **Multiple Morning Blocks:**
   - 4:45-5:30 (Dual Brand) → 5:30-6:30 (Body) → 6:45-8:00 (Software)
   - **Risk:** Cognitive fatigue by 8:00 AM
   - **Fix:** Ensure adequate rest between blocks, nutrition support

3. **Friday Evening Backend (7:30-9:00 PM):**
   - **Risk:** Mental fatigue after full day
   - **Fix:** Light dinner, hydration, energy management

4. **Saturday Revisions:**
   - Multiple revision blocks (1:30-5:00 PM)
   - **Risk:** Weekend burnout
   - **Fix:** Ensure Saturday morning rest, break between blocks

**Missing Information:**
- Sleep schedule not defined (cannot validate sustainability)
- Work schedule not defined (cannot check for conflicts)
- Recovery time not explicitly scheduled
